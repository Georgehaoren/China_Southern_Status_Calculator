For example, “First”, “First Class”, “Flagship First”, “Domestic First”, “Premium Economy”, “Pearl Economy”, “明珠经济舱”, “高端经济舱”, or similar labels may refer to different products depending on airline, route, aircraft, sales region, ticketing channel, and date. A label shown in this tool is a descriptive label only. It is not a guarantee that a flight will credit as that cabin, booking class, mileage rate, segment credit, lounge entitlement, baggage benefit, or elite benefit.
